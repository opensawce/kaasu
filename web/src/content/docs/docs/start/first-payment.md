---
title: Your first payment
description: Create an order, share the link and watch it confirm.
sidebar:
  order: 2
---

1. Create an order with an amount and an optional note.
2. Share the pay link or show the QR code.
3. The customer pays from any UPI app.
4. Your bank sends an alert. Kaasu checks it, matches it to the order and turns the order green.

Every order shows an icon, a colour and a word: **Confirmed**, **Waiting**, **Needs your OK**, **Rejected** or **Expired**. Colour is never the only signal.
