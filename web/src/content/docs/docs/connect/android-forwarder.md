title: Android forwarder
description: Pass bank SMS and app alerts to Kaasu.
sidebar:

---

The forwarder is a small Android app on a phone that receives your bank alerts. It signs each alert with a key unique to that phone and sends it to Kaasu.

- Pair it once with a code from the admin screen.
- You can revoke a phone at any time.
- Alerts older than 60 seconds or repeated are refused.
