---
title: Bank terms and your account
description: What to check with your bank before taking payments through UPI.
---

> **Draft.** This is practical guidance, not legal or tax advice.

- Use a current account for business collection. Some banks restrict personal accounts from business use.
- Check your UPI limits with your bank, per transaction and per day.
- Keep your bank statements. Kaasu's reconciliation compares against them.
- Kaasu never moves money. Refunds are made from your own bank app and then recorded here.
